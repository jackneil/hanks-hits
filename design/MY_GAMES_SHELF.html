Goal

A kid's own creations become first-class citizens in the app. Games marked madeByKid: true in their metadata.ts (the convention the make-a-game / remix-a-game skills already write) get a dedicated "My Games" shelf at the top of the home page with a personal-best stat per game, a "Games I Made" section on the profile page, and a warm empty-state invite when no creations exist yet. Today the app ignores the marker entirely: the runtime scan never parses it, DisplayItem never carries it, and no surface renders it.

Context: design/FRAMEWORK_ROADMAP.md "Top do-next" item 6 — the #1 missing stickiness surface. The skills half of the convention shipped already; this is the app half. Rider: SECURITY_BACKLOG.md item 2 (signup password minimum 6 → 8) folds into this branch.

Architecture

One source of truth — the madeByKid: true literal in each game's metadata.ts — flows to the two consumers through the repo's two existing metadata channels. No new state, no new storage.

Key decisions

DecisionChoice + why
Shelf placementBetween search and Recently Played — top billing for the product's core loop. Hidden while searching (same behavior as Recently Played); every shelf item also stays in its normal category, so search still finds it.
Dual listingA kid-made game appears on the shelf AND in its category. Removing it from the category would make games "disappear" depending on flags — confusing for a kid.
Empty stateThe shelf section always renders (except during search). With zero creations it shows a warm invite ("This shelf is waiting for YOUR first game!") — it advertises the platform's whole point.
Personal-best statClient-side from zustand-persisted localStorage. New shared helper findLocalProgress(appId) tries the repo's known key conventions (-storage, -progress, -save, -game-state, -state, hank- prefix), unwraps the persist envelope, and feeds extractGameStats. No stat found → a "New!" tag, never an error.
gameStatExtractor promotionMoves from apps/profile/lib/ to shared/lib/ — the shelf is its second consumer, which is exactly this repo's bar for promotion. Profile imports update; behavior unchanged.
Profile channelgenerate-game-metadata.ts adds madeByKid to the generated lookup, so the client-side profile filters it without new plumbing. Regenerated every build (existing caveat applies and is documented).
TestabilityparseMetadata becomes a named export of game-registry (pure function) for direct unit tests.
