VERDICT: NEEDS ATTENTION

- MEDIUM: Presence semantics are incomplete. Plan §“Recursive manifest grammar” lines 729-736 and 796 define `required`, `nullable`, and `missing:"empty"` without defining empty identities or required/missing precedence. Amend with explicit per-node empty values and composite presence rules, plus tests for optional and nullable paths such as `savedArtworks`, `currentEvent`, and `lastLocation`.

- MEDIUM: The grammar does not model real collection shapes. Lines 765-785 and 800 require numeric timestamp fields inside records, but achievements is `Record<string, number>` (`store.ts:7-10`, `progress-schemas.ts:720-729`), drawing/drum timestamps are ISO strings, and retro/joke/toy timestamp records are arrays. Platformer levels are keyed records (`platformer/store.ts:28-35`), while weather locations lack an `id` field (`weather/store.ts:11-17`). Add explicit keyed-record, array timestamp-record, ISO timestamp, and item-policy nodes, then map every affected path.

- MEDIUM: Raw/schema coverage permits false green stripping. Plan lines 483-500 and 814-817 only require paths to resolve through the manifest; they do not require observed raw/schema sets or an explicit disposition for raw-only paths. Drum emits `savedBeats[].patternLength` (`store.ts:128-135`, `396-404`), but the schema omits it (`progress-schemas.ts:639-646`) and Zod returns the stripped result (`:786-795`). Require observed-path derivation, exact set equality after declared exclusions, and either schema coverage or an explicit exclusion for every raw-only persisted path.

- MEDIUM: The policy matrix is not actually per-field. Lines 823-824 and 834-858 use unresolved categories such as “only source-proven,” “waves,” and “stats counters”; source-proven fields like Monster Truck `starsCollected` (`store.ts:448`, `550`) and Drum `stats.padsHit` (`store.ts:273`) are not named, while reserved `lastTick` and `updatedAt` are omitted from their rows. Replace prose with exact path-to-policy mappings and mandatory oracle cases for every leaf, including timestamp, opaque, excluded, reset, tie, and overflow behavior.
